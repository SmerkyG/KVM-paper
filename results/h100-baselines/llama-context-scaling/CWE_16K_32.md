# 16K CWE comparison

Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, BF16, batch one on H100. The 16K CWE validation split provides the same 32 dataset row indices as the original 32K comparison, using seed 42. It generates separate prompts and target words for those rows. The tokenized 16K prompts span 15,959–16,218 tokens. [Dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/self-long/RULER-llama3-1M) · [Original 32K comparison](DENSE_PREFILL.md#32k-ruler).

Both decoders use dense causal prefill. Squeezed keeps 2,900 centroids per KV head and calibrates p90 on 100 routing queries per prompt, following the 32K recipe. The fixed count gives about twice the nominal centroid density at 16K. Greedy generation has a 120-token cap and scoring stops at the first EOS/EOT. Both decoders match their own eager logits on the sampled check for every prompt.

| Decoder | Mean CWE score | Target-word hits | Zero-hit prompts |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Dense BF16 | 93.44% | 299 / 320 | 0 |
| Squeezed p90 | 94.06% | 301 / 320 | 0 |

Squeezed gains two target-word hits, or **+0.625 percentage points** (95% paired bootstrap interval -0.94 to +2.50, 100,000 resamples of prompt pairs). Squeezed wins on 5 prompts, dense on 3, and 24 tie. Whole-word scoring gives 301 hits for Squeezed and 299 for dense. The original 32K sample had 15 zero-hit prompts for dense and 9 for Squeezed.
